Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about NEXORA? Here are detailed answers to 16 common questions about our platform, mock tests, and study resources.

SPONSORED ADVERTISEMENT
1. PLATFORM ACCESS & REGISTRATION
Q1. What is NEXORA Educational Hub?
NEXORA is a unified digital platform that aggregates verified educational portals, live class links, study notes, and competitive exam preparation resources into one easy-to-use interface.
Q2. Do I need to pay or create an account to use NEXORA?
No, NEXORA is 100% free to access for students. You do not need to register an account or enter payment details to launch learning portals.
2. COURSES & STUDY MATERIAL
Q3. What types of courses and platforms are available on NEXORA?
NEXORA covers a wide range of platforms including competitive exam prep (JEE, NEET, UPSC), university lecture portals, board exam preparation, coding bootcamps, and skill development platforms.
Q4. How often are the learning links updated on NEXORA?
Our automated edge engine and content team update direct platform access links continuously to ensure zero downtime and 100% active links.
Q5. Where can I download PDF study notes?
Study notes and revision guides can be accessed by choosing the desired category on the homepage or clicking direct links provided inside educational cards.
Q6. Is the study material on NEXORA authentic and safe?
Yes, every educational link listed on NEXORA is thoroughly vetted and security-checked before publication.
3. MOCK TESTS & PRACTICE
Q7. Are mock tests available on NEXORA?
Yes, NEXORA provides direct access to online mock test portals and practice quiz modules designed for competitive entrance exams.
Q8. Can I track my test scores and progress?
Individual test scores and progress analytics are saved on the respective learning provider portals when you log into your student account.
4. PAYMENTS & ADVERTISEMENTS
Q9. Are there any hidden subscription fees on NEXORA?
No, NEXORA is completely free. We do not charge subscription fees to students or learners.
Q10. Why do I see advertisements on NEXORA?
We display Google AdSense advertisements to cover server maintenance and edge hosting costs, keeping NEXORA free for all students worldwide.
5. ACCOUNT & APP PREFERENCES
Q11. What is the Admin Console used for?
The Admin Console is reserved for platform administrators to configure new educational cards, update links, and manage Telegram popup notifications.
Q12. How do I save NEXORA as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on my phone?
Open NEXORA in your mobile browser (Chrome/Safari), tap the browser menu, and select 'Add to Home Screen' or 'Install App' for instant one-tap access.
6. TECHNICAL SUPPORT & COMPATIBILITY
Q13. Is NEXORA compatible with mobile devices and tablets?
Yes, NEXORA is built with a responsive mobile-first architecture that runs smoothly across Android smartphones, iPhones, iPads, tablets, and desktop browsers.
Q14. What should I do if a portal link fails to open?
If a link fails or returns a network error, click the 'Alternative Link' button inside the card or contact support via our Contact Us page.
7. CONTACT & SUGGESTIONS
Q15. How can I submit a new educational platform to be listed on NEXORA?
You can send us platform recommendations via our Contact Form with the platform name, URL, and category. Our admin team will review and list it.
Q16. How quickly does NEXORA support respond to inquiries?
Our dedicated support team reviews all inquiries and responds within 24 to 48 hours.
