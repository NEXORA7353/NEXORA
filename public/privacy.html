Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to us. This policy outlines how NEXORA collects, protects, and manages your personal and device data.

Last Updated: August 06, 2026

At NEXORA (accessible from https://nexora.pages.dev), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by NEXORA and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit NEXORA, we may collect the following information:

  • Log Files & Technical Data: Standard log file entries including Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser types, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring pages, and click counts.
  • Voluntary Form Submissions: Information provided voluntarily through our contact forms, such as your name, email address, and message content.
  • Local Storage Data: Preferences saved locally on your device (e.g. app category selection or Telegram modal dismissal state).

2. Use of Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, NEXORA uses 'cookies'. These cookies store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to www.website.com and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Analytics and Third-Party Links

NEXORA may utilize anonymized web analytics services to understand website traffic patterns and improve performance. Additionally, our platform provides outward links to third-party educational tools and websites. Please note that NEXORA has no control over or responsibility for the privacy practices of external websites.

5. Data Security

We implement robust security practices, including TLS/SSL encryption and secure HTTPS protocols, to safeguard all data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

6. Children's Information (COPPA Compliance)

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity. NEXORA does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately.

7. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to any personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your information. Contact us at privacy@nexora.pages.dev to exercise these rights.

8. Contact Information

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through our Contact Page or via email at support@nexora.pages.dev.
